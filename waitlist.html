The 4-Phase System

Identity → Alignment
→ Expression → Monetisation.

Each phase builds on the one before. Skip a phase and the whole thing wobbles. This is a non-negotiable rule — taught and enforced throughout the 10 weeks.

01
Weeks 1–2
Identity Blueprint

Excavate the lens only you see through. Values, perspective, lived experience, convictions. The internal architecture that makes your brand irreproducible.

02
Weeks 3–5
Market Alignment

Deep audience architecture. Positioning precise enough that the wrong person self-selects out. The messaging toolkit that sounds the same everywhere you show up.

03
Weeks 6–7
Content as Expression

Content pillars built from your identity. A weekly system that produces posts with a job, not noise with a schedule. Lead magnet designed as free proof of your system.

04
Weeks 8–10
Monetisation Architecture

One product, fully designed. Lead generation playbook. Conversion system built on earned trust, not pitch energy. Your complete brand launched publicly in Week 10.
